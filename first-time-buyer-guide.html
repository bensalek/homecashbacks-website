Hidden Costs to Prepare For

Most first-time buyers budget for the down payment and forget everything else.

Land Transfer Tax
Ontario charges LTT on a sliding scale. Toronto buyers pay twice, provincial and municipal, making it the largest closing cost. First-time buyers qualify for rebates. Use the closing cost calculator to see your exact amount before you make an offer.
Legal Fees
$1,200 to $1,400
Covers the title transfer, mortgage registration, and closing funds. Get a written estimate from your lawyer before signing anything.
Title Insurance
Protects against title defects and fraud. The cost scales with your purchase price. Ask your lawyer for a quote before closing.
Home Inspection
$400 to $600
A licensed inspector evaluates the structure and condition of the property. Book one as a condition of your offer on any resale home.
CMHC Mortgage Insurance
Required when your down payment is below 20%. Added to your mortgage, not due at closing. Factor it into your total borrowing cost when comparing down payment options.
Moving Costs
$800 to $3,000+
Professional movers charge by the hour. Book early and avoid end-of-month and weekend dates for the best rates.

Buying a Condo in Toronto

Condos are often the entry point for first-time buyers in Toronto. They come with considerations that freehold purchases do not.

Status Certificate
Your lawyer should review the status certificate before you firm up. It covers the building's finances, reserve fund, any active litigation, and rules. A thin reserve fund or lawsuit can affect your mortgage and resale value.
Maintenance Fees
Monthly fees in Toronto range from $400 to over $1,200 depending on the building. They cover maintenance, building insurance, and the reserve fund. High fees affect what a lender will approve you for.
New vs. Resale Condos
Pre-construction adds HST to the price and can close years after signing. Resale is move-in ready, has a known history, and can be inspected. Most first-time buyers are better served by resale.
Resale Potential
Not all condos appreciate equally. Small one-bedroom units in oversupplied buildings tend to underperform. Two-bedroom suites in well-managed buildings in walkable areas hold value better.
