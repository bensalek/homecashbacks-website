Common questions
Does HomeCashbacks charge the buyer anything? +
No. The $4,999 fee comes entirely from the buyer agent commission the seller already set aside in the MLS listing. You pay nothing out of pocket and nothing upfront. If you do not buy, you owe nothing beyond any showings after the first.
What if the seller offers less than $4,999 buyer agent commission? +
Our fee is capped at whatever commission the seller offered. We disclose the commission amount before you make any offer so there are no surprises at closing. In practice, most GTA listings offer 2 to 2.5% well above our flat fee on any home over $200,000.
Is the cashback taxable? +
Generally no. CRA treats a realtor cashback as a reduction in the purchase price of the home, not income. It does not appear on a T4 or need to be declared. Consult your accountant for your specific situation, particularly if you are purchasing an investment property.
What is the catch? +
There is not one. The business model works because we run lean no office, no buyer agent team, no expensive marketing. Laleh works directly with buyers who know what they want. The commission that traditional agents use to fund all of that overhead comes back to you instead. Same legal protections, same MLS access, same brokerage structure different fee.
What if I do not end up buying? +
You owe nothing except any showing fees after the first free one ($30 each). No commission, no cancellation fee, no pressure. The first showing is always free and there is no agreement required before that.
