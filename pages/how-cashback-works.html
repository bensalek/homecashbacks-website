Common questions
Does Home Cashbacks charge the buyer anything? +
No. The $4,999 fee comes from the buyer agent commission the seller already set aside. You pay nothing out of pocket. If you do not buy, you owe nothing beyond any showings after the first free one.
What if the seller offers less than $4,999? +
Our fee is capped at whatever the seller offered. We disclose the commission before you make any offer. In practice, most GTA listings offer 2 to 2.5% — well above our flat fee on any home over $200,000.
Is the cashback taxable? +
Generally no. CRA treats it as a reduction in the purchase price, not income. Consult your accountant for investment property purchases.
What is the catch? +
There is not one. We run lean — no office, no team, no expensive overhead. The commission traditional agents use to fund all of that comes back to you instead. Same legal protections, same MLS access, different fee.
What if I do not end up buying? +
You owe nothing except showing fees after the first free one ($30 each). No commission, no cancellation fee, no pressure.
