How a cashback realtor works in Toronto

How buyer cashback works in Ontario — step by step

When you buy a home in Ontario, the seller agrees upfront to pay a buyer agent commission — typically 2 to 2.5% of the sale price, built into the listing before you make any offer. That money flows to your agent at closing. Most agents keep all of it. We don't.

We charge a flat $4,999. Everything above that comes back to you.

The exact process

Here is how it flows from listing to cashback

01
Seller sets the buyer agent commission upfront
Before any offer is made, the seller specifies the buyer agent commission in the MLS listing — typically 2 to 2.5% of the sale price. On a $1M Toronto home that's $20,000 to $25,000. This is already priced into the deal whether you use a cashback realtor or not.
02
We represent you — showings, offers, negotiation, closing
Full buyer agent service. We book showings, advise on offer strategy, negotiate with the seller's agent, review conditions, and coordinate everything through to closing. Same service as a traditional Toronto realtor.
03
Your cashback is written into the contract before you sign
Before we show you a single property, the cashback amount is written into your buyer representation agreement with our brokerage. It is a contractual obligation — not a verbal promise, not a discount subject to conditions.
04
Deal closes — commission flows through the brokerage
At closing, the seller's proceeds fund the commission. It flows through the selling brokerage to ours. We take our flat $4,999 fee and process your cashback.
05
You receive your cashback 6 to 8 weeks after closing
Once the brokerage releases the funds, your cashback is sent directly to you. On the average GTA home price of $1,018,000 with a 2.5% buyer commission, that's approximately $20,446 back in your pocket.

Fully legal in Ontario

Yes, this is allowed. And it's in writing.

Buyer cashback is permitted under TRESA (Trust in Real Estate Services Act, 2002) and regulated by RECO. All transactions at HomeCashbacks are processed through Toronto Real Estate Realty Plus Inc., a licensed brokerage. The cashback amount is formally disclosed in your transaction documents.

Can a realtor walk it back after the deal closes? No. Your cashback is written into your representation agreement with the brokerage before we start working together. It is a contractual obligation, not a verbal promise. Once the deal closes and the commission is released, the brokerage processes the payment. There is no way to reverse it.

A fair question

Why don't other Toronto realtors offer this?

Because the current system works very well for them. A traditional buyer agent earns a percentage of whatever you pay. On a $1M home that can be $20,000 to $25,000, for the same work we do for $4,999. Most agents and brokerages have no interest in questioning a model that pays them that well. We do.

Today's buyers already do most of the work. You search on HouseSigma, shortlist on Realtor.ca, and often find the home before calling anyone. The commission model was built for a different era. We charge for what we actually do, and give back what we don't earn.
