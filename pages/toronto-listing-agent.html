Common questions

What sellers ask most

Is $4,999 the total commission I pay?
No. In Ontario, sellers also offer a buyer agent commission (typically 2 to 2.5% of the sale price) to attract buyer agents. That is separate from our $4,999 and goes to the buyer agent at closing. Our fee covers the listing side only - marketing, representation, negotiation, and closing support.
Will I get less for my home?
No. What gets you the best price is preparation, timing, and negotiation - not the listing fee. We deliver the same marketing and negotiation quality. The only difference is what you pay for it.
Why would a buyer agent show my home?
Buyer agents are required by RECO to show their clients properties that match their criteria regardless of the commission offered. Offering a competitive buyer agent commission (2 to 2.5%) is still recommended in Toronto to maximize showing traffic.
Is a flat listing fee legal in Ontario?
Yes. Listing commissions in Ontario are fully negotiable. There is no minimum set by RECO or any real estate board. You and your agent agree on the fee in your listing agreement.
What if my home does not sell?
You owe us nothing. Our $4,999 fee is due at closing only. No upfront fees, no cancellation fees, no obligation to relist with us.
