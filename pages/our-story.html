It started with our own home purchase. It didn't go the way we expected.

Laleh Mousavi
Realtor, Toronto Real Estate Realty Plus Inc.

Buying a home is one of the biggest financial decisions you will ever make.

I went through the process myself and what stayed with me was how much buyers carry on their own. So I tried to fix it. I started a startup company focused entirely on home buyers. I raised what I could, built what I could, and eventually had to close it. But the problem did not go away. So I became a realtor to finish what I started.

The problem did not go away. So neither did we.

My vision is to bring together every piece of the home buying process under one roof. Mortgage, legal, and everything in between, so you are not juggling ten different people.

I charge a flat fee because I believe the cost of buying a home has room to come down. Technology is changing what is possible and I want to pass those gains directly to you. Today's buyer is informed and does not need to be hand-held. What they need is the right support at the right moments.

When you close on your home, you should have money in your hand.

There is no catch. No commitment, no pressure, and no hard feelings if after a few showings this is not the right fit. Come in, see how it works, and decide for yourself.

Laleh
