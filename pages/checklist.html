About this checklist

How to use this home buyer checklist

Five phases, in order

This checklist follows the actual sequence of buying a home in Toronto and the GTA. Each phase builds on the last -- do not skip ahead. Buyers who skip pre-approval, for example, often fall in love with a home they cannot finance, which wastes time for everyone.

Phase 1: Finance

Get your mortgage pre-approval before you look at a single home. This tells you your actual budget, strengthens any offer you make, and prevents you from wasting time on homes outside your range. In competitive Toronto markets, sellers and their agents take pre-approved buyers more seriously.

Phase 2: Search

Use HouseSigma, Realtor.ca, or Zolo to browse listings. Send us the ones you want to see. We coordinate private showings across Toronto and the GTA -- your first showing is always free.

Phase 3: Offer

We review comparable sales, help you determine a fair offer price, and write the offer. All offer writing is free. We negotiate on your behalf and advise on conditions -- home inspection, financing, and status certificate for condos.

Phase 4: Closing

Once your offer is accepted, your lawyer handles the legal transfer. We coordinate with your lender, lawyer, and the listing agent to ensure a smooth closing. Budget for land transfer tax, legal fees, and title insurance -- our closing cost calculator can help.

Phase 5: Cashback

Your cashback is written into your buyer representation agreement before any offers are made. It arrives 6 to 8 weeks after closing once the commission is released from the brokerage trust account. On a $1M Toronto home you receive approximately $20,001 back.
