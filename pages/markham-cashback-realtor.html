Markham Neighbourhoods

Markham stretches from the heritage village of Unionville to the newer master-planned communities of Cornell and Cathedraltown. Each area has a distinct character and price point.

Heritage village
Unionville
One of the most charming and consistently in-demand addresses in the GTA. Heritage main street, excellent schools, and a tight-knit community. Properties move quickly and rarely sit.
Detached from ~$1.8M
Tech hub
Downtown Markham
Emerging mixed-use core with newer condos, office towers, and improving walkability. Appeals to tech workers and buyers wanting a more urban feel without downtown prices.
Condos from ~$600K
Family planned
Cornell
Master-planned community in north Markham with newer homes, parks, and top school options. Consistent demand from families upgrading from Scarborough and North York.
Detached from ~$1.2M
Upscale suburban
Berczy Village
Newer builds on generous lots with a strong community feel. Popular with families who want modern construction and good schools at prices below Unionville.
Detached from ~$1.3M
Value townhomes
Milliken Mills
Townhouses and semis with good Highway 407 access. One of Markham's more accessible entry points for first-time buyers who want York Region schools.
Towns from ~$700K
Heritage charm
Cathedraltown
Architecturally distinctive newer community with European-inspired design. Detached homes on quiet streets at prices that remain below the Markham average.
Detached from ~$1.1M
