The headline number

Prices are down, not up

As of August 2026, the GTA-wide average selling price was $993,410, down 2.7% year-over-year and down from July 2026's $1,003,956. New listings fell 14.1% year-over-year, a sharper decline than the 2.1% drop in sales, and months of supply rose from 4.4 to 4.8. Taken together, this remains a market that favours buyers more than it has in recent years, not a market heating back up.

What this actually means

For buyers and sellers right now

For buyers, this remains a market with genuine negotiating room, rising months of supply and softening prices mean less pressure to waive conditions or bid over asking than in recent years. For sellers, accurate pricing from day one matters more than ever, an overpriced listing sits even longer in a market where buyers already have more options and less urgency.
