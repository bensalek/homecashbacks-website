Data as of July 2026, TRREB Market Watch

The headline number

Prices are down, not up

As of July 2026, the GTA-wide average selling price was $1,003,956, down 4.5% year-over-year and down from June 2026's $1,058,928. Days on market rose to 45, up from 40 in June, and the sale-to-list ratio sat at 97%. Taken together, this is a market that currently favours buyers more than it has in recent years, not a market heating back up.

What this actually means

For buyers and sellers right now

For buyers, this is a market with genuine negotiating room, longer average days on market and softening prices mean less pressure to waive conditions or bid over asking than in recent years. For sellers, accurate pricing from day one matters more than ever, an overpriced listing sits even longer in a market where buyers already have more options and less urgency.
