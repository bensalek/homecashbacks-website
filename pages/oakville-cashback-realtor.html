The Oakville Market Right Now

Oakville is consistently ranked among the best places to live in Canada and its real estate market reflects that reputation. Lakefront access, top schools, strong GO Transit service, and a genuine town centre make it one of the most complete communities in the GTA. Prices are among the highest outside Toronto proper, but 2026 has seen inventory rise and buyer competition ease.

Oakville Neighbourhoods

Oakville covers a wide range of price points from Old Oakville lakefront estates to the newer subdivisions of north Oakville. Understanding which pocket suits your budget and lifestyle is essential before you start.

Lakefront prestige
Old Oakville
Heritage homes, tree-lined streets, a walkable downtown, and direct lake access. One of the most complete neighbourhoods in the GTA. Consistently strong demand and limited supply.
Detached from ~$2.5M
GO Transit access
Kerr Village
Walkable, artsy, and undervalued relative to Old Oakville. Close to the GO station and the lake. Semis and renovated bungalows at prices well below the Oakville average.
Mixed from ~$900K
Top schools
River Oaks
Established family neighbourhood with some of Oakville's best public schools. Large lots, mature trees, and consistent demand from families moving up from Mississauga.
Detached from ~$1.5M
New builds
North Oakville
Master-planned communities with newer construction, parks, and good highway access. More affordable than south Oakville while sharing the same school board.
Detached from ~$1.1M
