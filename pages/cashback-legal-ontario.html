The money flow

How it actually works

When a seller lists a home in Ontario, they specify the buyer agent commission upfront in the MLS listing. This is an explicit amount stated before any buyer sees the property. The buyer pays nothing for their agent. The seller sets and funds it.

A cashback realtor receives exactly what the seller offered, takes a fixed fee, and sends the rest back to you. The money never changes hands illegally it flows from the seller through the brokerage trust account to the agent, and from the agent to you, with everything documented before closing.

The regulation

What RECO actually says

RECO the Real Estate Council of Ontario is the regulatory body that licenses every real estate agent in Ontario. Their rules are clear on this.

RECO Code of Ethics
A registrant may rebate, refund, or give credit for a portion of their remuneration to a client or customer, provided the rebate or other benefit is disclosed in writing.
Real Estate and Business Brokers Act, 2002 Ontario Regulation 580/05

The key phrase is "disclosed in writing." That is the only requirement. The cashback must be documented not kept off the record, not paid in cash on the side. Everything is transparent and on paper.

The honest answer

Why most agents don't offer it

It is not because it is illegal. It is because it is not in their financial interest. A traditional agent on a $1M home earns $25,000. Giving back $20,000 of that requires a fundamentally different business model lower volume fees, higher transaction volume, and the belief that transparency wins in the long run.

Protect yourself

What to watch out for

Cashback realtors are legitimate but not all operators are equal. Three things to verify before signing anything.

Verbal promises
If the cashback is not written into your buyer representation agreement before you start viewing properties, it does not legally exist. Get it in writing from day one.
Unlicensed operators
Anyone offering cashback must be a RECO-registered agent operating under a licensed brokerage. Check the RECO public registry before signing anything.
Cashback tied to conditions
Some operators reduce or eliminate your cashback if you don't close within a specific timeframe or use their preferred mortgage broker. Read the agreement carefully.
