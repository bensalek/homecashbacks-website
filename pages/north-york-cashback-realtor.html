North York is one of the GTA's most diverse and transit-connected housing markets. The Yonge-Sheppard corridor anchors condo demand while areas like Willowdale and Bayview Village draw freehold buyers priced out of central Toronto. North York Neighbourhoods

North York covers a wide range of price points and property types. Willowdale and Bayview Village trade at premium prices for good reason. Don Mills and Lawrence Manor offer more value. Knowing which pocket fits your budget before you start looking saves weeks.

Most in demand
Willowdale
The Yonge-Sheppard hub. Dense condo inventory, strong transit, and walkable retail. Popular with first-time buyers and downsizers. Values here have held well through market cycles.
Condos from ~$600K
Prestige freehold
Bayview Village
Quiet, tree-lined streets, top schools, and some of North York's most sought-after freehold stock. Move-up buyers from across the city target this area. Prices reflect it.
Detached from ~$2M

Don Mills
One of Toronto's original planned communities. Good schools, ravine access, and freehold prices well below comparable Bayview addresses. Solid long-term value.
Detached from ~$1.2M

Lawrence Manor
West of Bathurst, Lawrence Manor offers semi-detached and detached homes at prices below the North York average. Getting attention from buyers who want freehold without the premium.

At the top of the Yonge corridor, Newtonbrook offers freehold and condo options with easy TTC access. A quieter alternative to Willowdale with similar connectivity.
