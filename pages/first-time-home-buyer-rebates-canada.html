First-Time Home Buyer Rebates Canada

Every federal and Ontario program in one place for 2026, what's actually free money versus your own savings with tax perks, and how cashback stacks on top of all of it.

The short answer

Five programs, and they're not all the same kind of benefit

First-time buyers in Canada can access five main programs. Two of them, the FHSA and the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan, give you tax-sheltered access to your own money, not free cash. The other three, the Home Buyers' Amount, the GST/HST rebate, and the Ontario and Toronto land transfer tax rebates, are genuine, non-repayable savings. Both types matter, but it's worth knowing which is which before you plan your budget around them.

Your own money, tax-sheltered

FHSA and the RRSP Home Buyers' Plan

The First Home Savings Account (FHSA) combines the best of an RRSP and a TFSA. Contributions are tax-deductible, up to $8,000 per year to a $40,000 lifetime maximum, and withdrawals for a first home are completely tax-free, growth included. To make a qualifying withdrawal, you generally need a written purchase agreement in place by October 1 of the year after you withdraw.

The RRSP Home Buyers' Plan (HBP) lets you withdraw up to $60,000 from your RRSP toward a first home, with no tax owing at withdrawal. The catch: it must be repaid to your RRSP over 15 years, usually starting the second year after you withdraw. Miss a scheduled repayment and that amount gets added to your taxable income for the year.

The two can be combined for the same purchase, up to $100,000 in tax-advantaged funds for one buyer, or up to $200,000 for a couple buying together, each using their own FHSA and HBP room.

A real tax credit

The Home Buyers' Amount

Also called the Home Buyers' Tax Credit, this lets a first-time buyer claim $10,000 on line 31270 of their federal tax return in the year they buy, worth up to $1,500 in actual tax relief. It's non-refundable, meaning it reduces tax you owe rather than generating a cheque, and any unused portion can't be carried forward to a future year.

On new construction

The GST/HST New Housing Rebate

If you're a first-time buyer purchasing a new construction home, the federal GST/HST New Housing Rebate can eliminate GST entirely on homes priced up to $1,000,000, worth up to $50,000, with the benefit phasing out on homes between $1,000,000 and $1,500,000. Ontario also has a separate, temporary enhanced version of this rebate currently in effect, worth up to $130,000 combined on homes up to $1,500,000, open to all buyers, not just first-timers.
