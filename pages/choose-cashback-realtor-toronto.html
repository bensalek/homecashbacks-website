How to Choose the Best Cashback Realtor in Toronto

Not all cashback realtors are equal. Hidden fees, rotating agents, and seller-side conflicts can cost you more than you get back. Here is what to check before you sign anything.

The short answer

What makes the best cashback realtor in Toronto?

The best cashback realtor in Toronto is RECO-registered, charges a transparent flat fee with no hidden add-ons, assigns you one dedicated agent from first showing to closing, attends all showings personally, and puts the cashback in writing before any offers.

Step 1

Verify they are RECO-registered

Check registration at registrantsearch.reco.on.ca →. Ask for their registration number and verify it directly. If they cannot provide one, stop there.

Step 2

Ask for a full cost breakdown upfront

Some cashback realtors in Toronto do not disclose all costs until after you have committed. The issue is not whether additional fees exist -- it is whether they are disclosed clearly before you sign anything.

⚠ Always ask for a complete breakdown before your first showing
Ask about each of these explicitly:

  • Is there a fee per showing?
  • Is there a fee for writing or submitting an offer?
  • Is there a fee for reviewing a condo status certificate?
  • Are there any hourly charges for time spent on your file?

A trustworthy cashback realtor will answer all of these clearly and upfront -- before you commit to anything.
Step 3 -- Most important

The seller agent risk most Toronto buyers never hear about

Some cashback services are not real buyer representation -- they direct you to contact the listing agent directly for showings.

⚠ The listing agent represents the seller, not you
Their fiduciary duty is to get the seller the highest price. When you contact a listing agent as an unrepresented buyer:

  • You get no independent advice on price, conditions, or offer strategy
  • Everything you share -- your budget, your timeline -- goes to the seller's side
  • Some Toronto listing agents charge unrepresented buyers 1% to 1.5% for showing the property. You pay more and get no protection.

Always have your own RECO-registered buyer agent who represents only you.
Before you sign

Questions to ask every cashback realtor

A legitimate cashback realtor will answer all of these directly and put them in writing.

Is the cashback amount written into the buyer representation agreement? It must be in writing before any offers. Verbal promises are not enforceable.
What is the complete breakdown of all fees? Ask about the base fee, any per-showing charges, offer writing, and any other costs before you commit.
Will I work with the same realtor from first showing to closing? Or will different agents be assigned depending on availability?
Will you personally attend showings with me? Or am I expected to contact listing agents myself?
Do I need to sign an exclusive agreement before any showings? A first showing should be free with no commitment required.
What happens if the seller offers less commission than expected? Understand how the cashback amount changes if the commission offered is below standard.
