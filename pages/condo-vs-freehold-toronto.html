Condo vs. Freehold

Not a question with one right answer, a question about what trade-offs you're actually willing to make. Here's how to think through it honestly.

The short answer

It's a trade-off, not a ranking

Neither option is objectively better. A condo trades some control and space for predictability and lower maintenance effort. A freehold home trades that predictability for full control and, usually, more space per dollar outside the core. The right choice depends on your budget, how much hands-on maintenance you want to deal with, and how much you value flexibility versus predictability.

Current market context

This isn't a fixed rule, it shifts with the market

As of July 2026, Toronto's condo apartment benchmark price was down 7.35% year-over-year, a sharper decline than the broader market. Freehold segments have generally held value better through the same period. This reflects current supply and demand conditions, not a permanent truth about condos versus houses. A genuinely useful way to decide

Ask yourself these questions honestly

A condo probably fits better if...
You'd rather budget a predictable fee than handle surprise repair costs
You value location and walkability over square footage
You have no interest in lawn care, snow removal, or exterior upkeep
Amenities like a gym or concierge genuinely matter to your daily life

A freehold home probably fits better if you want full control over your property, more space and a yard, and you're comfortable managing and budgeting for maintenance yourself, including the occasional large, unplanned expense.
