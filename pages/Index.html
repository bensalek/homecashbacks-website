We get it

This sounds too good to be true.

This seems too good to be true. Why don't other realtors do this?

Most don't because they don't have to. The traditional commission on a $1M Toronto home is $25,000 -- that money covers brokerage splits, referral fees, office costs, and yes, the expensive cars you see on real estate Instagram. We run lean. No middlemen, no overhead. We charge for the actual work and return the rest. The commission was always your money.

If you charge less, will I get worse service?

No. We personally attend every showing, write every offer, and are your single point of contact from first showing to closing. You are not passed to a coordinator or a rotating team. The fee is lower because we run lean -- not because anything is cut.

How do I know I can trust this?

First -- RECO-registered and verifiable at registrantsearch.reco.on.ca

Second -- your cashback is written into the buyer representation agreement before any offers. A legal commitment, not a promise.

Third -- real clients. Real reviews. 5 stars on Google.
