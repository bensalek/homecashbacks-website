Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026  |  Laleh Mousavi, Realtor, Toronto Real Estate Realty Plus Inc. operating as HomeCashbacks

This Privacy Policy explains how HomeCashbacks, operated by Laleh Mousavi, Realtor, registered with Toronto Real Estate Realty Plus Inc. ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through our website. We are committed to complying with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario privacy legislation.

1. What information we collect

When you submit a contact form on our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number

We do not collect payment information, government ID, or any sensitive personal data through this website.

2. Why we collect it

We collect this information solely to respond to your inquiry — to follow up about booking a showing or answering questions about our services. We do not use your information for automated profiling or targeted advertising.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you in response to your inquiry
  • To schedule showings or consultations you have requested
  • To send you relevant follow-up information related to your home purchase

We will not use your information for unrelated marketing without your explicit consent.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal information with any third parties. Your information is used solely to respond to your inquiry and will not be disclosed to any outside organization.

5. How long we keep your information

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to complete your inquiry or transaction, or for a maximum of 2 years if no transaction is completed. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. How we protect your information

Contact form submissions are transmitted securely and stored in access-controlled systems. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent to our use of your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

Our website does not currently use cookies, analytics tracking, or third-party advertising scripts. If this changes, this policy will be updated accordingly.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. For material changes that affect how we collect, use, or share your personal information, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you directly — for example by email if we have your contact information. Continued use of our website does not constitute consent to material changes. If you do not agree with any changes, you may request deletion of your information by contacting us at the address in Section 10.

10. Contact us

For privacy-related inquiries, access requests, or deletion requests:

Laleh Mousavi — HomeCashbacks, Toronto Real Estate Realty Plus Inc.
Email: Laleh@homecashbacks.ca
Subject line: "Privacy Request"

11. Regulatory oversight

Our real estate services are regulated by the Real Estate Council of Ontario (RECO) under the Trust in Real Estate Services Act, 2002 (TRESA). For concerns about our real estate conduct, you may contact RECO at reco.on.ca. For privacy complaints, you may contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada at priv.gc.ca.
