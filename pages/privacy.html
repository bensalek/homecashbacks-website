Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026  |  Laleh Mousavi, Realtor, Smart Brokerages operating as HomeCashbacks

This Privacy Policy explains how HomeCashbacks, operated by Laleh Mousavi, Realtor, registered with Smart Brokerages ("we", "us", "our"), collects, uses, and protects personal information submitted through our website. We are committed to complying with the Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) and applicable Ontario privacy legislation.

1. What information we collect

When you submit a contact form on our website, we collect:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Any details you choose to share about what you're looking for

When you browse our website, we also collect information about your visit, such as the pages you view, how you arrived at our site, and the date and time of your visits. This is collected through cookies, as described in Section 8.

We do not collect payment information, government ID, or any sensitive personal data through this website.

2. Why we collect it

We collect this information to respond to your inquiry, to follow up about booking a showing or answering questions about our services, and to understand which topics are most relevant to you so our follow-up is useful. We do not sell your information or use it for targeted advertising.

3. How we use your information

  • To contact you in response to your inquiry
  • To schedule showings or consultations you have requested
  • To send you relevant follow-up information related to your home purchase
  • To understand which pages and topics you've shown interest in, so we can tailor our follow-up to your needs

We will not use your information for unrelated marketing without your explicit consent.

4. Who we share it with

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing purposes. If you specifically request a related service we don't provide directly, such as a mortgage pre-approval, we may share your contact information with a trusted partner (for example, a mortgage broker we work with) solely to fulfill that request. We share only what is necessary for that purpose, and only when you have asked for that service.

We use trusted service providers to operate our business, including HubSpot (customer relationship management), Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity (website analytics), our website host, and our email providers. These providers process your information on our behalf and only for the services they provide to us. Some of them store information on servers outside Canada, including in the United States, where it may be accessible to courts, law enforcement, and national security authorities under the laws of that country.

5. How long we keep your information

We retain your contact information for as long as necessary to complete your inquiry or transaction, or for a maximum of 2 years if no transaction is completed. You may request deletion at any time (see Section 7).

6. How we protect your information

Contact form submissions are transmitted securely and stored in access-controlled systems. We take reasonable precautions to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. However, no internet transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your rights

Under PIPEDA, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Withdraw consent to our use of your information at any time

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at the email address below. We will respond within 30 days.

8. Cookies and tracking

Our website uses Google Analytics to understand general visitor behaviour, such as which pages are viewed and how visitors navigate the site. Google Analytics uses cookies to collect this information, which is aggregated and does not identify you personally. Google Analytics is provided by Google LLC. You can learn about how Google collects and processes data at policies.google.com/privacy, and you can opt out of Google Analytics tracking using the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

We also use HubSpot, a customer relationship management (CRM) service provided by HubSpot, Inc. HubSpot places cookies on your browser to record the pages you visit on our website. If you submit a contact form, HubSpot links that browsing activity, including visits made before you submitted the form, to the contact details you provide, and we may be notified when you return to our website. This helps us understand what you're interested in so we can follow up with relevant information. You can learn how HubSpot handles data at legal.hubspot.com/privacy-policy.

We also use Microsoft Clarity, provided by Microsoft Corporation, to understand how visitors use our website. Clarity uses cookies to record how you move, click, and scroll on our pages, including session recordings and heatmaps, so we can find and fix parts of the site that are confusing or hard to use. Clarity masks text you type into forms, so recordings do not capture your name, email, or phone number. You can learn how Microsoft handles this data at privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

You can block or delete cookies at any time through your browser settings. Our website will still work if you do, but we won't be able to connect your visits to your inquiry. We do not use third-party advertising scripts or targeted advertising cookies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page will reflect any changes. For material changes, we will make reasonable efforts to notify you directly. Continued use of our website does not constitute consent to material changes.
