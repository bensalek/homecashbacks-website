Etobicoke is one of Toronto's most underrated markets. It offers lakeshore access, established freehold neighbourhoods, and consistent transit links -- at prices that remain below comparable central Toronto areas. Inventory has risen in 2026 and buyer competition has softened, making this a strong window for serious buyers.

Etobicoke Neighbourhoods

Etobicoke runs from The Kingsway in the north to Long Branch on the lakeshore. Each pocket trades differently. Knowing which area matches your budget and lifestyle before you start looking saves time and money.

Most prestigious
The Kingsway
One of Toronto's most established addresses. Large lots, mature trees, and proximity to both Bloor West Village and the Humber River. Premium pricing reflects consistent demand.
Detached from ~$2M
