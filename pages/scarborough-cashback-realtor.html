Scarborough Neighbourhoods

Scarborough is large and varied. The Bluffs corridor is completely different from Agincourt. Knowing which pockets offer the best value for your budget and commute requirements matters before you start booking showings.

Best overall value
Clairlea
Semi-detached and detached homes at some of the most accessible prices in Toronto. Strong community, good transit links, and consistent buyer interest. A genuine entry point to Toronto freehold ownership.

Established neighbourhood with access to the Bluffs trails and waterfront parks. Detached homes here offer good square footage at prices well below comparable east-end neighbourhoods.

Guildwood
One of Scarborough's most desirable addresses. Ravine lots, Bluffs walking trails, and a strong GO station. Prices reflect the quality of life. Buyers who find it tend to stay.
